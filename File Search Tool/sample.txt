Robert Graves (1895–1985) was a British poet, novelist, and scholar. He is widely known for his historical novels, particularly "I, Claudius" and "Claudius the God." Graves also wrote extensively on mythology, including the influential book "The White Goddess."

Throughout his life, Robert Graves published more than 140 works spanning poetry, fiction, and literary criticism. He served in World War I and his experiences influenced much of his early poetry.

His writing style is admired for its narrative strength, imaginative storytelling, and deep engagement with classical history and myth.
